Delete your Meal Factory account

You can delete your account and all your data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Meal Factory and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to Help & account and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your data is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email sahupriyanshu69@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Meal Factory account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (goal, body details, diet, allergies, budget, targets) and your login.
  • All meal plans, food logs, created and saved recipes, AI usage records and feedback you sent.

What may remain

  • Server logs for up to 30 days (for security), then deleted.
  • Water and recent-recipe data stored only on your phone: removed when you delete your account in the app, or when you uninstall it.
