Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Meal Factory Android app and this website.

Meal Factory ("we", "us") is built by Priyanshu Sahu and Chirag Jaiswar in India. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and how you can delete it. Questions: sahupriyanshu67@gmail.com.

What we collect

From your Google sign-in

  • Your name, email address and Google account ID. We never see your Google password.

What you tell the app

  • Your goal (for example lose weight or gain muscle), gender, age, height, current and target weight, timeline and activity level.
  • Diet type, food allergies, cuisine preferences, daily food budget and daily calorie target.
  • Meal plans, foods you log (including text you type, such as "2 rotis and dal"), recipes you create or save, and how many AI features you used each day.
  • Feedback you send from inside the app.

Kept only on your phone

  • Water glasses for each day and your list of recently generated recipes. These never leave your device.

Technical data

  • Server logs (time, the screen or feature called, IP address, errors) used to keep the service secure and fix bugs.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or payment card details, and we do not show ads.

How we use it

  • To calculate your calorie and protein targets and plan meals that fit your goal, diet, allergies and budget.
  • To show your progress, food logs and saved recipes across sessions.
  • To limit daily AI use fairly, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To read your feedback and improve the app.

AI processing

To create meal plans, recipes, swaps and nutrition estimates, our server sends the details needed for that request (such as your goal, calorie and protein targets, diet, allergies, cuisine, budget and the food text you typed) to AI model providers: Google (Gemini API), Groq and OpenRouter (which routes requests to the model's provider). We do not send your name or email to them. On free plans, some of these providers may keep requests for a limited time or use them to improve their services, under their own terms. AI answers are checked by our server for your allergies and diet, but please always double-check ingredients if you have a serious allergy.

Who processes your data for us

  • Supabase: Google sign-in and login sessions.
  • MongoDB Atlas: our database (your profile, plans, logs, recipes, feedback).
  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run, India region): runs our servers.
  • Google, Groq, OpenRouter: AI processing as described above.
  • Unsplash: dish photos (only the dish name is sent, never personal data).
  • Google (Gmail): delivers feedback and system alerts to our team.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we share it only with these service providers, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data: until you delete your account.
  • Cached AI answers: deleted automatically within days.
  • Server logs: kept for up to 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your goal, diet, allergies, budget and targets any time in the app (Profile, Your plan, Edit).
  • Delete your account and all your data in the app: Profile, Delete account. Or follow these steps.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing sahupriyanshu67@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may also raise a concern with the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the database and AI keys is limited to our servers and team, and every request is checked against your signed-in account.

Children

Meal Factory is not meant for children under 13. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Health

Meal Factory gives general nutrition estimates, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian about medical conditions, pregnancy or eating disorders.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when the change is important.
